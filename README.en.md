**eBrand** helps shop owners and small businesses in Vietnam get found, win the right customers and cut repetitive work with websites, marketing and AI. We find the real bottleneck first, test a small scope, measure before and after, and only then scale.

## What we do

| Area | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Websites & landing pages** | Company sites, sales landing pages, call/Zalo/form tracking, small internal tools |
| **Getting found** | Website SEO, Google Maps SEO, Vietnamese SEO content |
| **Marketing & sales** | Facebook Pages & Ads, sales scripts, lead tracking and follow-up schedules |
| **AI sales video** | Insight-led scripts, AI-assisted editing, one long video into many short ones |
| **AI agents & automation** | 24/7 assistants grounded in approved documents, human handoff, logged workflows |

**AI products:** eBrandAI Writer · eBrandAI Skills · AI Sales Agent.

## How we work

<p align="center"><img src="assets/how-we-work.svg" alt="How we work: Talk → Diagnose → Small pilot → Measure and scale" width="100%"></p>
